# Concepts

* [Operational Schedule Pressure ("Go Fever") & Manifest Compression](go-fever-schedule-pressure.md) - Informal organizational compulsion to meet ambitious launch manifests or milestone deadlines, shifting the burden of proof from proving it is safe to fly to proving it is unsafe.
* [Violation of "Test as You Fly, Fly as You Test" Principle](inadequate-flight-like-test-as-you-fly.md) - Omitting integrated system tests after hardware reworks, voltage changes, or polarity-sensitive assembly (e.g., MPL leg-deployment test re-run omission; Genesis pre-launch centrifuge test waiver).
* [Normalization of Deviance (Vaughan Sociological Model)](normalization-of-deviance.md) - Gradual institutional process whereby repeated anomalous flight events (O-ring char/erosion on 15 prior flights; bipod foam shedding on 7 prior flights) are reclassified as acceptable bounded risk.
* [Siloed Software/Hardware Interface Control Document (ICD) Governance](siloed-interface-control-documents.md) - Lack of end-to-end automated verification across contractor-agency interface boundaries, allowing unit mismatches or inverted CAD coordinate conventions to bypass peer review.
* [Single-Instrument Metrology Reliance & Ignored Refractive Null Discrepancy](single-vendor-metrology-blindspot.md) - Relying solely on a single custom reflective null corrector while dismissing conflicting spherical aberration indications from a lower-precision refractive null corrector without independent verification.
* [Communication Filtering of Dissenting Engineering Judgments](suppressed-dissenting-engineering-opinion.md) - Management hierarchy dynamics in which contractor or working-level engineering objections (e.g., Thiokol low-temperature teleconference; STS-107 DoD on-orbit imaging requests) fail to reach flight readiness decision-makers.
