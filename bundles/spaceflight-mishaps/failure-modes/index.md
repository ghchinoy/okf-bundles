# Concepts

* [Submerged Cryo-Heater Detent Switch Welding & PTFE Insulation Ignition](cryo-tank-teflon-ignition.md) - Thermostatic switches rated for 28V DC welded closed during a 65V ground detanking procedure, baking Tank 2 above 500 °C and stripping Teflon fan-wire insulation.
* [16.7 psi Pure-Oxygen Cabin Flash Fire Propagation](high-pressure-pure-oxygen-flash-fire.md) - Rapid deflagration of Velcro, nylon netting, and polyurethane foam in a 100% oxygen atmosphere pressurized above sea-level pressure, preventing inward-opening hatch egress.
* [4.45x lbf·s vs. N·s Small-Forces Impulse Unit Conversion Mismatch](imperial-metric-impulse-unit-mismatch.md) - Ground software file AMD output thruster impulse in English pound-force seconds while navigation software expected SI newton-seconds, biasing periapsis altitude down to 57 km.
* [180-Degree Inverted Deceleration G-Switch Orientation](inverted-g-switch-sensor-installation.md) - Internal sensing masses were oriented away from the reentry deceleration vector due to ambiguous mechanical drawing symmetry, preventing contact closure during atmospheric entry.
* [Unfiltered Hall-Effect Touchdown Sensor Latching During Leg Deployment](premature-hall-effect-touchdown-cutoff.md) - Flight control software failed to flush latched Hall-effect touchdown bits generated when landing legs unfolded at 40 m altitude, cutting descent engines in midair.
* [Wing Leading Edge RCC Panel 8 Kinetic Impact Breach & Spar Burn-Through](rcc-leading-edge-kinetic-breach.md) - A 0.76 kg foam suitcase shed at Mach 2.46 struck the lower lip of RCC Panel 8 at 850 km/h, opening a breach through which superheated reentry plasma melted the aluminum wing spar.
* [1.3-mm Reflective Null Corrector Field Lens Misspacing](reflective-null-corrector-spacing-error.md) - Laser metering rod touched an anodized aluminum cap whose anti-reflective paint had flaked off, causing the field lens to be shimmed 1.3 mm too far and grinding the mirror flat at the edges.
* [Viton Fluorocarbon O-Ring Low-Temperature Glass-Transition Resiliency Loss](viton-oring-low-temp-extrusion-blowby.md) - At 28 °F (-2.2 °C), Viton O-ring elastomer hardened and failed to extrude into the widening tang-and-clevis gap within the 600 ms ignition pressure rise, allowing 3,000 °C gas blow-by.
