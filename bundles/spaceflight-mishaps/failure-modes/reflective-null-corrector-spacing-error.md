# 1.3-mm Reflective Null Corrector Field Lens Misspacing

Laser metering rod touched an anodized aluminum cap whose anti-reflective paint had flaked off, causing the field lens to be shimmed 1.3 mm too far and grinding the mirror flat at the edges.
