# Concepts

* [Redesigned Solid Rocket Motor (RSRM) Capture-Feature Tang, Third O-Ring & Joint Heaters](captured-feature-triple-oring-joint-heater.md) - Post-Challenger SRB field-joint redesign adding an interference-fit capture tang to prevent joint rotation, a third O-ring, and thermostatically controlled 75 °F joint strip heaters.
* [COSTAR Corrective Optics Space Telescope Axial Replacement & Independent Optical Cross-Checks](costar-corrective-axial-optics.md) - Deployment of coin-sized inverse-aberration M1/M2 mirrors on STS-61 to restore diffraction-limited HST imaging, paired with mandatory dual-independent optical test paths for future flagship telescopes.
* [Independent Technical Authority (ITA) & Formal Dissenting Opinion Escalation Path](independent-technical-authority-ita.md) - Post-CAIB governance reform funding Chief Engineer technical authorities independently of program schedule budgets and establishing non-retaliatory dissenting engineering review boards.
* [Block II 60/40 N2-O2 Launch Pad Atmosphere & Outward-Opening Quick-Release Hatch](nitrogen-oxygen-cabin-atmosphere-redesign.md) - Post-Apollo 1 redesign replacing pure oxygen on the pad with a 60% nitrogen / 40% oxygen mixture, Beta-cloth fireproof insulation, and a 5-second outward-opening unified hatch.
* [Orbiter Boom Sensor System (OBSS), Bipod Ramp Elimination & Rendezvous Pitch Maneuver](on-orbit-tps-laser-boom-inspection.md) - Post-Columbia removal of External Tank bipod foam ramps (replaced by electric rod heaters), mandatory Flight Day 2 OBSS laser/camera wing leading-edge inspection, and ISS R-Bar pitch flip.
* [Mandatory SI Metric Interface Typing & Hardware-in-the-Loop End-to-End Polarity Checks](strict-si-unit-icd-automated-verification.md) - Enforcement of strongly typed SI physical units across flight/ground software pipelines and mandatory physical stimulus polarity tests for all guidance and landing sensors.
