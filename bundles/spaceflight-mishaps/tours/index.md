# Concepts

* [Tour: Invisible Math, Inverted Sensors, and Metrology Flaws in Deep Space](tour-invisible-math-and-metrology-bugs-in-deep-space.md) - Follows how subtle interface and test omissions—a 4.45x imperial/metric mismatch on Mars Climate Orbiter, an inverted G-switch on Genesis, unushed Hall sensors on Mars Polar Lander, and a 1.3-mm paint-flake spacing error on Hubble—cascaded into major spaceflight emergencies.
* [Tour: Normalization of Deviance — From Challenger O-Rings to Columbia Foam](tour-normalization-of-deviance-challenger-columbia.md) - Investigative narrative tracing how repeated survivable anomalies (O-ring erosion on STS-51-L and bipod foam shedding on STS-107) became institutionally normalized until catastrophic boundary conditions were crossed.
