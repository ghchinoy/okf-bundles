# Concepts

* [Accountable and Transparent](accountable-and-transparent.md) - Availability of information about an AI system to interacting stakeholders across the lifecycle, paired with clear human accountability.
* [Explainable and Interpretable](explainable-and-interpretable.md) - Representation of the mechanisms underlying AI systems operation and the meaning of their outputs in the context of their designed functional purposes.
* [Fair — with Harmful Bias Managed](fair-with-harmful-bias-managed.md) - Addressing systemic, computational, statistical, and human-cognitive biases across the AI lifecycle to promote equity and prevent discriminatory harm.
* [Privacy-Enhanced](privacy-enhanced.md) - Safeguarding human autonomy, identity, and dignity via technical and policy controls such as differential privacy and data minimization.
* [Safe](safe.md) - AI systems should not under defined conditions lead to a state in which human life, health, property, or the environment is endangered.
* [Secure and Resilient](secure-and-resilient.md) - AI systems can withstand adversarial attacks and unexpected perturbations, and maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
* [Valid and Reliable](valid-and-reliable.md) - Confirmation through objective evidence that requirements for a specific intended use or application have been fulfilled, and the ability of an item to perform as required over time.
