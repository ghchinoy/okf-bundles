# Concepts

* [GOVERN 1: Policies and Procedures](govern-1.md) - Policies, processes, procedures, and practices across the organization related to the mapping, measuring, and managing of AI risks are in place, transparent, and implemented effectively.
* [GOVERN 2: Accountability Structures](govern-2.md) - Accountability structures are in place so that the appropriate teams and individuals are empowered, responsible, and trained for mapping, measuring, and managing AI risks.
* [GOVERN 3: Workforce Diversity & Inclusion](govern-3.md) - Workforce diversity, equity, inclusion, and accessibility processes are prioritized in the mapping, measuring, and managing of AI risks throughout the lifecycle.
* [GOVERN 4: Risk-Aware Organizational Culture](govern-4.md) - Organizational teams are committed to a culture that considers and communicates AI risk.
* [MANAGE 1: Risk Prioritization & Response](manage-1.md) - AI risks based on assessments from MAP and MEASURE functions are prioritized, responded to, and managed.
* [MANAGE 2: Benefit Maximization & Impact Mitigation](manage-2.md) - Strategies to maximize AI benefits and minimize negative impacts are planned, prepared, implemented, and documented.
* [MANAGE 3: Third-Party & Supply Chain AI Risk](manage-3.md) - AI risks and benefits from third-party entities, pre-trained models, and datasets are managed across the supply chain.
* [MAP 1: Context Established](map-1.md) - Intended purpose, potentially beneficial uses, context-specific laws, norms, and deployment environment are understood and documented.
* [MAP 2: AI System Categorization](map-2.md) - Categorization of the AI system is performed based on technical capabilities, autonomy level, and risk profile.
* [MAP 3: Capabilities and Limitations](map-3.md) - AI capabilities, targeted usage, goals, and expected benefits and costs compared with appropriate benchmarks are understood.
* [MEASURE 1: Methods and Metrics](measure-1.md) - Appropriate quantitative and qualitative methods and metrics are identified and applied to evaluate AI risks.
* [MEASURE 2: Trustworthiness Evaluation](measure-2.md) - AI systems are evaluated for trustworthy characteristics: validity, safety, security, privacy, fairness, and explainability.
* [MEASURE 3: Continuous Risk Tracking](measure-3.md) - Mechanisms for tracking identified AI risks over time in production settings are in place.
