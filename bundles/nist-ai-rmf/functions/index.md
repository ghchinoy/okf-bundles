# Concepts

* [GOVERN](govern.md) - Cultivate and implement a culture of risk management throughout the organizations designing, developing, deploying, evaluating, or acquiring AI systems.
* [MANAGE](manage.md) - Allocate risk resources to mapped and measured risks on a regular basis and as defined by the GOVERN function.
* [MAP](map.md) - Establish the context to frame risks related to an AI system across the lifecycle.
* [MEASURE](measure.md) - Employ quantitative, qualitative, or mixed-method tools, techniques, and methodologies to analyze, assess, benchmark, and monitor AI risk and related impacts.
