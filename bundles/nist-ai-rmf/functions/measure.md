# MEASURE Function

The **MEASURE** function employs quantitative, qualitative, or mixed-method tools and methodologies to analyze, assess, benchmark, and monitor AI risk identified during [map](/functions/map.md) under the oversight of [govern](/functions/govern.md). Its metrics feed into [manage](/functions/manage.md) and sanctions like [demographic-parity-ratio](/computations/demographic-parity-ratio.md) and [calibration-drift-score](/computations/calibration-drift-score.md).

## Core Categories
- [measure-1](/categories/measure-1.md) — Appropriate methods and metrics are identified and applied.
- [measure-2](/categories/measure-2.md) — AI systems are evaluated for trustworthy characteristics.
- [measure-3](/categories/measure-3.md) — Mechanisms for tracking identified AI risks over time are in place.
