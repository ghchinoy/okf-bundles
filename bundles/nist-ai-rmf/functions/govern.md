# GOVERN Function

The **GOVERN** function is cross-cutting and informs every other function of the NIST AI RMF ([map](/functions/map.md), [measure](/functions/measure.md), and [manage](/functions/manage.md)). It establishes organizational policies, processes, accountability structures, and culture for AI risk management.

## Core Categories
- [govern-1](/categories/govern-1.md) — Policies, processes, procedures, and practices across the organization related to the mapping, measuring, and managing of AI risks are in place, transparent, and implemented effectively.
- [govern-2](/categories/govern-2.md) — Accountability structures are in place so that the appropriate teams and individuals are empowered, responsible, and trained for mapping, measuring, and managing AI risks.
- [govern-3](/categories/govern-3.md) — Workforce diversity, equity, inclusion, and accessibility processes are prioritized in the mapping, measuring, and managing of AI risks throughout the lifecycle.
- [govern-4](/categories/govern-4.md) — Organizational teams are committed to a culture that considers and communicates AI risk.
