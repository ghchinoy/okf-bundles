# MAP Function

The **MAP** function establishes context to frame risks related to an AI system. Governed by [govern](/functions/govern.md), the outcomes of MAP directly inform scientific evaluation in [measure](/functions/measure.md) and risk treatment in [manage](/functions/manage.md).

## Core Categories
- [map-1](/categories/map-1.md) — Context is established and understood.
- [map-2](/categories/map-2.md) — Categorization of the AI system is performed.
- [map-3](/categories/map-3.md) — AI capabilities, targeted usage, goals, and expected benefits and costs compared with appropriate benchmarks are understood.
