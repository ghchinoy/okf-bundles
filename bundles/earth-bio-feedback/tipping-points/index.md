# Concepts

* [Amazon Canopy Evapotranspiration Recycling Collapse & Savannization](amazon-rainforest-savannization-dieback.md) - Regional hydrological tipping point where deforestation and drought break the westward atmospheric moisture-recycling cascade, converting tropical rainforest into fire-prone savanna.
* [Stommel Salt-Advection Feedback & AMOC Bistable Shutdown Threshold](amoc-thermohaline-bistable-collapse.md) - Nonlinear salt-advection tipping point in which Greenland meltwater freshening halts North Atlantic deep-water convection, flipping the overturning circulation into a weak/off state.
* [Coral–Symbiodiniaceae Thermal Bleaching & Aragonite Undersaturation Tipping Point](coral-reef-thermal-bleaching-threshold.md) - Dual thermal and chemical tipping point in which Degree Heating Weeks > 8 °C-weeks expel endosymbiotic zooxanthellae while falling aragonite saturation impedes reef calcification.
* [Irreversible Decompensated Shock Positive-Feedback Spiral (Guyton Curve Crossover)](decompensated-hemorrhagic-shock-cascade.md) - When acute blood loss exceeds baroreceptor and RAAS compensation, myocardial ischemia depresses cardiac output, further lowering coronary perfusion in a lethal positive-feedback cycle.
* [Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA) Runaway Lipolysis & Acidotic Spiral](diabetic-ketoacidosis-runaway-lipolysis.md) - Pathophysiological breakdown of glycemic negative feedback: absolute insulin deficiency and unopposed glucagon/cortisol trigger runaway hormone-sensitive lipase ketogenesis, osmotic diuresis, and metabolic acidosis.
* [Marine Ice Sheet Instability (MISI Retrograde Grounding-Line Retreat)](west-antarctic-marine-ice-sheet-instability.md) - Cryospheric tipping point occurring on retrograde submarine bedrock slopes where grounding-line retreat increases ice-flux thickness across the grounding zone in a self-sustaining loop.
