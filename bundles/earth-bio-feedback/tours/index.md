# Concepts

* [Tour: Walter Cannon's Wisdom of the Body — Glycemic & Hemodynamic Homeostasis vs. Runaway Shock](tour-human-homeostasis-vs-runaway-pathology.md) - Walks how antagonistic hormonal and autonomic negative feedback loops (Insulin–Glucagon and Carotid Baroreceptors + RAAS) keep blood glucose and arterial pressure stable, and what happens when positive-feedback tipping points (DKA and decompensated shock) take over.
* [Tour: Planetary Thermostats vs. Amplifying Cascades — Earth's Climate Feedback Graph](tour-planetary-thermostats-and-tipping-points.md) - Explores the tug-of-war between Earth's stabilizing negative feedbacks (Planck blackbody radiation and the multimillion-year Urey silicate weathering thermostat) and fast amplifying feedbacks (ice-albedo, water vapor, permafrost methane) leading to tipping points.
* [Tour: Isomorphic Buffer Chemistry — From Blood Hemoglobin & Bicarbonate to Ocean Acidification](tour-universal-cybernetics-of-earth-and-body.md) - Cross-domain synthesis comparing carbonic-acid buffering in human arterial blood (Bohr/Haldane effect) with the surface ocean carbonate buffer (Revelle factor), coral bleaching thresholds, and synthetic PID control.
