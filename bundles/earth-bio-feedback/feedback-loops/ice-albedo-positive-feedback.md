# Cryosphere Ice–Albedo Positive Feedback Loop (+0.3 to +0.4 W/m2/K)

Self-reinforcing positive feedback in which warming melts high-albedo sea ice and snow (alpha ~ 0.8), exposing dark ocean or tundra (alpha ~ 0.1) that absorbs more solar energy.
