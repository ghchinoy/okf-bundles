# Concepts

* [Carotid Sinus & Aortic Arch Baroreceptor Autonomic Negative Feedback Loop](baroreceptor-arterial-pressure-reflex.md) - Beat-to-beat mechanoreceptor reflex arc via the nucleus tractus solitarii (NTS) modulating vagal parasympathetic and sympathetic vasomotor tone to stabilize mean arterial pressure.
* [Reciprocal Bohr & Haldane Hemoglobin Allosteric Gas-Transport Loop](bohr-haldane-hemoglobin-allostery.md) - Coupled molecular feedback where metabolically active acidic/high-CO2 tissues right-shift the O2-hemoglobin curve to unload O2 (Bohr), while pulmonary O2 oxygenation unloads CO2 (Haldane).
* [Terrestrial CO2 Fertilization vs. Soil Respiration Thermal Crossover Loop](co2-fertilization-stomatal-feedback.md) - Biphasic feedback in which elevated CO2 initially enhances C3 plant water-use efficiency and carbon uptake (negative feedback) until heat stress and soil respiration reverse the sink.
* [Cryosphere Ice–Albedo Positive Feedback Loop (+0.3 to +0.4 W/m2/K)](ice-albedo-positive-feedback.md) - Self-reinforcing positive feedback in which warming melts high-albedo sea ice and snow (alpha ~ 0.8), exposing dark ocean or tundra (alpha ~ 0.1) that absorbs more solar energy.
* [Dual-Hormone Push–Pull Insulin–Glucagon Negative Feedback Loop](insulin-glucagon-glycemic-homeostasis.md) - Classic physiological negative-feedback control loop maintaining plasma glucose within a narrow band via antagonistic beta-cell insulin and alpha-cell glucagon secretion.
* [Permafrost Thaw & Thermokarst Methanogenesis Positive Feedback Loop](permafrost-methane-clathrate-feedback.md) - Positive biogeochemical loop where Arctic amplification thaws permafrost active layers, enabling microbial decomposition into CO2 and CH4 (28–36x 100-year GWP), accelerating warming.
* [Stefan–Boltzmann Planck Outgoing Longwave Radiative Damping (-3.2 W/m2/K)](planck-blackbody-radiative-damping.md) - Fundamental stabilizing negative feedback of planetary climate: as surface temperature T rises, outgoing thermal radiation increases as sigma*T^4, restoring radiative equilibrium.
* [Clausius–Clapeyron Water Vapor & Tropospheric Lapse-Rate Feedback](water-vapor-lapse-rate-feedback.md) - Fast atmospheric positive feedback in which a warmer troposphere holds ~7% more water vapor per Kelvin (Clausius–Clapeyron relation), roughly doubling CO2-alone radiative forcing.
