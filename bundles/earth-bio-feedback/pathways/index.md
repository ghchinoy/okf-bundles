# Concepts

* [Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC Thermohaline Conveyor)](atlantic-meridional-overturning-circulation.md) - Density-driven global ocean conveyor in which warm saline Gulf Stream waters cool and sink in the North Atlantic, sequestering heat and anthropogenic carbon at depth.
* [Alpha-Cell Glucagon & Hepatic Glycogenolysis / Gluconeogenesis Pathway](hepatic-glycogenolysis-gluconeogenesis.md) - Counter-regulatory catabolic pathway activated during fasting or hypoglycemia, mobilizing hepatic glycogen and synthesizing glucose from lactate, glycerol, and alanine.
* [Hypothalamic–Pituitary–Adrenal (HPA) CRH-ACTH-Cortisol Neuroendocrine Axis](hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal-axis.md) - Three-tier neuroendocrine cascade (CRH -> ACTH -> Cortisol) mobilizing stress energy substrates while exerting delayed negative feedback on the hypothalamus and anterior pituitary.
* [Marine Phytoplankton Biological & Carbonate Counter-Pump](marine-biological-carbon-pump.md) - Photosynthetic fixation of DIC by surface diatoms and coccolithophores followed by gravitational export of particulate organic carbon ("marine snow") into the mesopelagic and abyssal ocean.
* [Pancreatic Beta-Cell ATP/K+ Sensing & GLUT4 Insulin Translocation Cascade](pancreatic-beta-cell-insulin-cascade.md) - Anabolic endocrine pathway where elevated plasma glucose closes K_ATP channels, depolarizing beta cells to secrete insulin and trigger skeletal-muscle/adipose GLUT4 uptake.
* [Renin–Angiotensin–Aldosterone System (RAAS) Volume & Pressure Homeostasis](renin-angiotensin-aldosterone-system.md) - Renal juxtaglomerular enzymatic cascade converting angiotensinogen to Angiotensin II and stimulating adrenal aldosterone to restore extracellular fluid volume and arterial pressure.
* [Urey Silicate-Carbonate Geochemical Weathering Thermostat](silicate-carbonate-weathering-cycle.md) - Multimillion-year negative feedback pathway in which warmer, wetter climates accelerate carbonic-acid weathering of continental CaSiO3 rocks, burying CaCO3 on the seafloor.
* [Chapman Ozone-Oxygen Photochemical Cycle & Catalytic Montreal Recovery](stratospheric-ozone-chapman-cycle.md) - Stratospheric UV-photolysis pathway continuously interconverting O2 and O3, absorbing biologically lethal UV-B/C radiation and damping stratospheric thermal gradients.
