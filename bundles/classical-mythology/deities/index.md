# Concepts

* [Apollo](apollo.md) - Olympian god of music, prophecy, truth, healing, the sun, and archery; twin brother of Artemis and patron of Delphi.
* [Artemis (Diana)](artemis.md) - Olympian goddess of the hunt, the wilderness, wild animals, and the moon; twin sister of Apollo.
* [Athena (Minerva)](athena.md) - Virgin goddess of wisdom, handicraft, and strategic warfare, sprung fully armed from the forehead of Zeus.
* [Hades (Pluto)](hades.md) - Lord of the Underworld and ruler of the dead; brother of Zeus and Poseidon.
* [Hera (Juno)](hera.md) - Queen of the Olympian gods, goddess of marriage, women, childbirth, and family, and sister-wife of Zeus.
* [Hermes (Mercury)](hermes.md) - Herald and messenger of the Olympian gods, guide of souls to the Underworld (Psychopompos), and patron of travelers.
* [Poseidon (Neptune)](poseidon.md) - Olympian god of the sea, storms, earthquakes, and horses; brother of Zeus and Hades.
* [Zeus (Jupiter)](zeus.md) - King of the Olympian gods, ruler of Mount Olympus, and god of the sky, lightning, thunder, law, and order.
