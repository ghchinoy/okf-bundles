# Concepts

* [Achilles](achilles.md) - Greatest warrior of the Achaeans in the Trojan War, son of Peleus and the sea-nymph Thetis, central figure of Homer's Iliad.
* [Heracles (Hercules)](heracles.md) - Divine hero, son of Zeus and Alcmene, famed for his extraordinary strength and the completion of the Twelve Labors.
* [Jason](jason.md) - Leader of the Argonauts on the voyage of the Argo to recover the Golden Fleece from Colchis.
* [Odysseus (Ulysses)](odysseus.md) - King of Ithaca renowned for his intellect, cunning, the Trojan Horse stratagem, and his ten-year homeward voyage.
* [Perseus](perseus.md) - Legendary founder of Mycenae and slayer of the Gorgon Medusa, aided by Athena and Hermes.
