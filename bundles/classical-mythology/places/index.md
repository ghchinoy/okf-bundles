# Concepts

* [Oracle of Delphi](delphi.md) - Sacred precinct on Mount Parnassus where the Pythia delivered prophecies inspired by Apollo.
* [Ithaca](ithaca.md) - Ionian island kingdom of Odysseus, Penelope, and Telemachus, destination of the Odyssey.
* [Mount Olympus](mount-olympus.md) - Highest mountain in Greece and throne of the Twelve Olympian deities ruled by Zeus and Hera.
* [Troy (Ilium)](troy.md) - Ancient fortified city in Anatolia besieged by the Achaeans during the ten-year Trojan War.
* [The Underworld (Hades)](underworld.md) - Realm of the dead bounded by the rivers Styx and Acheron, ruled by Hades and Persephone.
