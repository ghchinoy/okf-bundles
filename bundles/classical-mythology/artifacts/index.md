# Concepts

* [The Aegis](aegis.md) - Divine shield or breastplate borne by Zeus and Athena, mounted with the head of the Gorgon Medusa given by Perseus.
* [The Golden Fleece](golden-fleece.md) - Fleece of the gold-haired winged ram Chrysomallus, symbol of kingship and authority sought by Jason and the Argonauts.
