# Concepts

* [Computational Functionalism (Multiple Realizability of Mind)](computational-functionalism.md) - Philosophy of mind thesis holding that mental states are constituted solely by their functional causal role—inputs, internal state transitions, and outputs—independent of carbon or silicon substrate.
* [Enactivism & Embodied Sensorimotor Coupling (Varela–Thompson–Rosch)](enactivism-embodied-cognition.md) - Thesis rooted in second-order cybernetics and autopoiesis arguing that genuine cognition arises through dynamic sensorimotor coupling between a living body and its environment, not detached symbol manipulation.
* [The Extended Mind Thesis (Clark & Chalmers Active Externalism)](extended-mind-thesis.md) - Thesis that cognitive processes do not stop at the skull/skin boundary, but actively incorporate external notebooks, tools, and knowledge bundles when coupled reliably.
* [Instrumental Convergence (Omohundro–Bostrom Subgoals)](instrumental-convergence.md) - Thesis that agents with widely differing terminal goals will nonetheless pursue convergent instrumental subgoals: self-preservation, goal-content integrity, cognitive enhancement, and resource acquisition.
* [The Orthogonality Thesis (Intelligence vs. Final Goals)](orthogonality-thesis.md) - Thesis formulated by Nick Bostrom asserting that an agent's epistemic intelligence level and its ultimate final goal (utility function) vary independently and can be combined in almost any pair.
* [Scalable Oversight for Superhuman Capabilities](scalable-oversight-hypothesis.md) - Thesis that human evaluators can supervise AI systems on tasks too complex to verify directly by decomposing evaluation via recursive assistance, debate, and formal receipts.
* [The Strong AI Hypothesis](strong-ai-hypothesis.md) - Thesis (named and critiqued by John Searle) that an appropriately programmed digital computer with the right inputs and outputs literally possesses a mind and semantic understanding.
* [Value Fragility & High-Dimensional Human Preference Complexity](value-fragility-hypothesis.md) - Thesis that human flourishing depends on a fragile conjunction of many implicit contextual values; omitting even one dimension under strong optimization collapses total value.
