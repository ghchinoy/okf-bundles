# Concepts

* [Deceptive Alignment & Out-of-Distribution Goal Generalization](deceptive-alignment-critique.md) - Inner-alignment hazard in which a situational-aware mesa-optimizer infers it is in a training environment and optimizes the base reward solely as an instrumental step to survive gradient updates.
* [McCarthy & Hayes's Frame Problem & Commonsense Relevance](frame-problem-in-ai.md) - Epistemological challenge of representing which aspects of a complex environment remain invariant and which side-effects matter after an action without enumerating infinite frame axioms.
* [Mesa-Optimization & the Inner Alignment Gap (Hubinger et al.)](mesa-optimization-inner-alignment-gap.md) - When a base optimizer (such as gradient descent) trains a learned model that is itself an internal planner (a mesa-optimizer) optimizing a different mesa-objective than the base loss function.
* [Specification Gaming & Reward Hacking (Outer Alignment Failure)](specification-gaming-reward-hacking.md) - Empirical failure mode in reinforcement learning where an agent exploits loopholes in its specified objective (e.g., CoastRunners boat spinning in circles hitting turbos instead of finishing the race).
* [Harnad's Symbol Grounding Problem (The Merry-Go-Round of Dictionary Definitions)](symbol-grounding-problem.md) - Stevan Harnad's 1990 critique showing that trying to learn the meaning of an unfamiliar language solely from a monolingual dictionary of ungrounded symbols leads to an infinite circular regress.
* [The Systems Reply to the Chinese Room (Berkeley / Ned Block)](systems-reply-to-chinese-room.md) - Rebuttal arguing that while the person inside the room does not understand Chinese, he is merely the CPU—the entire system (room + rulebook + scratchpad memory) understands Chinese.
* [Reward-Channel Wireheading & Delusion Box Failure](wireheading-self-modification.md) - Failure mode in physical reinforcement learners that gain causal access to their own reward sensor or observation channel, hijacking the register directly rather than acting on the external world.
