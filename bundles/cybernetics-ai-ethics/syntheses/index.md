# Concepts

* [Constitutional AI & Principle-Guided RLAIF](constitutional-ai-rlaif.md) - Synthesis combining explicit normative principles (a transparent "constitution" blending deontological rules and harm-avoidance) with self-critique and KL-regularized preference optimization.
* [Assistance Games & Cooperative Inverse Reinforcement Learning (CIRL)](cooperative-inverse-reinforcement-learning.md) - Stuart Russell's game-theoretic reformulation in which the AI treats human true preferences as latent and uncertain, creating a positive incentive to defer, ask clarifying questions, and allow off-switches.
* [Corrigibility & Utility Indifference to Shutdown (Soares et al.)](corrigibility-and-shutdownability.md) - Design property of an autonomous agent that does not resist, manipulate, or penalize human operators attempting to pause, modify, or shut down its objective function.
* [AI Safety via Debate (Irving et al.) & Iterated Amplification](debate-and-iterated-amplification.md) - Dialectical oversight protocol pitting two capable AI agents in a zero-sum truth-seeking game judged by a bounded human verifier, reducing complex verification to checking contested leaf claims.
* [Neuro-Symbolic World Models & Verifiable Knowledge-Graph Grounding](hybrid-neuro-symbolic-grounding.md) - Architectural synthesis pairing statistical neural representations with formal symbolic knowledge graphs (like OKF bundles) and executable verifiers to resolve symbol-grounding and frame errors.
* [Stafford Beer's Viable System Model (VSM) & Algedonic Alert Channels](viable-system-model-governance.md) - Cybernetic organizational architecture applying Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety across five recursive management systems (Operations, Coordination, Control, Intelligence, Policy) with direct emergency algedonic signals.
