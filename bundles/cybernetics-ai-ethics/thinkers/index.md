# Concepts

* [Alan M. Turing (1912–1954)](alan-turing.md) - Founder of theoretical computer science and author of "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950), introducing the Universal Turing Machine and the Imitation Game.
* [John R. Searle](john-searle.md) - Philosopher of mind and language who formulated the Chinese Room Argument (1980) and biological naturalism, distinguishing intrinsic intentionality from observer-relative syntax.
* [Nick Bostrom](nick-bostrom.md) - Philosopher and author of "Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies" (2014), formalizing the Orthogonality Thesis, Instrumental Convergence, and the Paperclip Maximizer.
* [Norbert Wiener (1894–1964)](norbert-wiener.md) - Mathematician and founder of Cybernetics (1948) and author of "The Human Use of Human Beings" (1950) and "Some Moral and Technical Consequences of Automation" (1960), warning of literal-minded optimization.
* [Stuart J. Russell](stuart-russell.md) - Computer scientist and co-author of "Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach" and "Human Compatible" (2019), replacing fixed-objective optimization with Cooperative Inverse Reinforcement Learning.
* [W. Ross Ashby (1903–1972)](w-ross-ashby.md) - Cybernetician and psychiatrist, inventor of the Homeostat and author of "Design for a Brain" (1952) and "An Introduction to Cybernetics" (1956), formulating the Law of Requisite Variety.
