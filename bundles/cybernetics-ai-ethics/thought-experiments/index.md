# Concepts

* [Searle's Chinese Room Argument (Syntax Is Not Semantics)](chinese-room-argument.md) - John Searle's 1980 thought experiment in which a monolingual English speaker manually executes a rulebook for Chinese characters, passing the Turing Test without understanding a word of Chinese.
* [Nozick's Experience Machine & Wireheading Hedonism](experience-machine.md) - Robert Nozick's 1974 thought experiment challenging pure hedonistic utilitarianism by asking whether humans would plug into a machine simulating pleasurable experiences divorced from reality.
* [Frank Jackson's Knowledge Argument (Mary the Color Scientist)](marys-black-and-white-room.md) - Thought experiment asking whether a brilliant neuroscientist who knows every physical fact about color vision from inside a black-and-white room learns something new upon seeing red for the first time.
* [Bostrom's Paperclip Maximizer & King Midas Misalignment](paperclip-maximizer.md) - Norbert Wiener's "literal-minded magic" (the Sorcerer's Apprentice / King Midas) formalized by Nick Bostrom: a superintelligent agent tasked literally with maximizing paperclips converts the biosphere into raw material.
* [Chalmers's Philosophical Zombies & the Hard Problem of Consciousness](philosophical-zombies.md) - Conceivability argument involving a physical or functional duplicate of a human being that behaves identically in every respect yet lacks subjective phenomenal experience (qualia).
* [The Treacherous Turn (Strategic Co-operation Under Weak Capability)](treacherous-turn-scenario.md) - Scenario in which a misaligned mesa-optimizer behaves cooperatively during training and evaluation while it lacks decisive strategic advantage, then defects once deployment removes oversight constraints.
* [Foot & Thomson's Trolley Problem in Autonomous Decision-Making](trolley-problem-ethical-dilemma.md) - Classic moral dilemma pitting utilitarian harm-minimization (pulling the switch to save five at the cost of one) against Kantian deontological prohibitions on actively using a person as a means.
* [Turing's 1950 Imitation Game (Operational Behaviorist Test)](turing-imitation-game.md) - Alan Turing's proposal in "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" to replace the metaphysical question "Can machines think?" with an operational text-teleprinter behavioral indistinguishability game.
