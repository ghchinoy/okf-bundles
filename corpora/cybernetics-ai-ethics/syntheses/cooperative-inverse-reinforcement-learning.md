# Assistance Games & Cooperative Inverse Reinforcement Learning (CIRL)

Stuart Russell's game-theoretic reformulation in which the AI treats human true preferences as latent and uncertain, creating a positive incentive to defer, ask clarifying questions, and allow off-switches.
