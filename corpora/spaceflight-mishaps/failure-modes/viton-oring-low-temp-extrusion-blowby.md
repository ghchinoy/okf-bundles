# Viton Fluorocarbon O-Ring Low-Temperature Glass-Transition Resiliency Loss

At 28 °F (-2.2 °C), Viton O-ring elastomer hardened and failed to extrude into the widening tang-and-clevis gap within the 600 ms ignition pressure rise, allowing 3,000 °C gas blow-by.
