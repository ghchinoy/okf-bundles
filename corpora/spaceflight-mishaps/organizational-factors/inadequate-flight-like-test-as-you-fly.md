# Violation of "Test as You Fly, Fly as You Test" Principle

Omitting integrated system tests after hardware reworks, voltage changes, or polarity-sensitive assembly (e.g., MPL leg-deployment test re-run omission; Genesis pre-launch centrifuge test waiver).
